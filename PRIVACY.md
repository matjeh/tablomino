# Privacy Policy — Tablomino

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

Tablomino collects **no personal data, of any kind, from anyone, ever.**

- Everything the app stores — player profiles, practice progress, badges,
  settings — is saved only on your own device, in a private SQLite database
  belonging to the app. Tablomino never transmits it anywhere.
- There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. The app has no backend to
  send data to.
- There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no
  third-party SDKs of any kind embedded in the app.
- The app makes no network requests at all. Everything it needs is bundled
  inside the app itself, so it works fully offline.

This applies equally to children using the app: Tablomino does not collect,
store, or share any personal information about any user, child or adult,
under any circumstance.

## Device backups

If you have device backup switched on, your phone or tablet may include
Tablomino's data in that backup — exactly as it does for your other apps.
This is done by Android (Google One / Google Drive) or by iOS (iCloud),
into **your own** account. Tablomino has no access to those backups and
receives nothing from them. You can turn backups off, or exclude
Tablomino from them, in your device's own settings.

The practical benefit is that your child's progress can follow you to a
new phone or tablet. Note that this only works between devices of the
same kind — an Android backup restores to Android, an iCloud backup to
iOS — so it does not move progress between an Android device and an
iPhone or iPad.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all stored information from your device. If
your device backup included Tablomino, a copy may remain in your own
Google or Apple account until that backup expires or you delete it there.
