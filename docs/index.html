Privacy Policy — Tablomino

Last updated: 2026-07-25

Tablomino collects no personal data, of any kind, from anyone, ever.

  • Everything the app stores — player profiles, practice progress, badges, settings — is saved only on your own device, in a private SQLite database belonging to the app. None of it is transmitted anywhere.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. The app has no backend to send data to.
  • There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind embedded in the app.
  • The app makes no network requests at all. Everything it needs is bundled inside the app itself, so it works fully offline.

This applies equally to children using the app: Tablomino does not collect, store, or share any personal information about any user, child or adult, under any circumstance.

Uninstalling the app removes all stored information immediately and completely, since nothing exists anywhere else.
