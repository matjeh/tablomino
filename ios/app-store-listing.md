## English (primary)

**Subtitle** (30 chars max): `Times tables, made fun`

**Promotional text** (170 chars max, editable without re-review):
> Free, offline, no ads, no accounts. Kalk the robot helps your kid master
> addition, subtraction, multiplication and division — one table at a time.

**Description** (4000 chars max):
> Tablomino helps children aged 6 to 11 memorize their addition, subtraction,
> multiplication and division tables through a spaced repetition system (the
> Leitner method) that automatically adapts to each child: facts they haven't
> mastered yet come back more often, while mastered facts are spaced out
> further so review time stays focused on what actually needs practice.
>
> FREE. NO ADS. NO TRACKERS. 100% LOCAL.
> Tablomino collects no personal data of any kind, from anyone, ever. There's
> no account, no sign-in, and no server — everything runs and stays on your
> device, and the app works fully offline. This is open source software, so
> that promise doesn't rely on trust alone: anyone can verify it in the code.
>
> WHAT'S INSIDE
> • All four operations — addition, subtraction, multiplication, division —
>   with configurable difficulty and question count
> • A 4-box spaced-repetition engine that quietly tracks what each child
>   knows and adapts future sessions accordingly
> • A visual progress grid per operation, so kids can see their tables fill
>   in as they improve
> • Badges for table mastery, streaks, and lifetime milestones
> • Multiple local profiles — perfect for siblings sharing one device
> • 8 languages: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese,
>   Chinese, and Arabic (with full right-to-left layout)
>
> Whether you're a parent looking for a genuinely private app for your kid,
> or a teacher recommending offline practice, Tablomino is built to be a
> tool you don't have to think twice about.

**Keywords** (100 chars max, comma-separated, no spaces):

**What's New in This Version** (v1.0.0):
> Initial release: addition, subtraction, multiplication and division
> practice with spaced repetition, multiple profiles, badges, and support
> for 8 languages including Arabic.
